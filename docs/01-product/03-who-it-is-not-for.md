# Who it is not for

Saying no keeps the first production release shippable.

## Not the primary user

- Ticket buyers
- The general public
- Artists using a consumer fan app
- Emergency services as a command-and-control client
- Hospitals
- Social-media managers
- Sales CRMs

Emergency services may later receive a credential and a restricted view. They are not the first buyer and not the first user.

## Not in the first production release

- Public website / ticket shop
- Native iOS or Android apps
- Offline-first sync
- Voice-to-incident
- Public emergency SMS
- AI that writes incidents or makes decisions
- Full artist rider and hotel management
- Full fundraising CRM and Gift Aid filing
- CCTV video streaming
- Radio voice
- Payroll
- Accounting

Those items have later-phase files. Do not pull them forward because they are interesting.

## Adjacent tools we do not replace

| Tool | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| Radio | Stays. Livehelm records outcomes. |
| WhatsApp | Should shrink. Do not integrate as the source of truth. |
| Excel | Import/export later. Not the live picture. |
| Ticket platforms | Attendance figure can be typed or later imported. |
| Weather services | We display and threshold. We do not become a meteorologist. |
| CAD / emergency-service systems | Out of scope. |

## Feature requests that are usually a no

- “Can it also sell tickets?”
- “Can it also be our company intranet?”
- “Can AI just run control?”
- “Can we livestream CCTV into the dashboard?”
- “Can volunteers download a social app?”

Take them as later-phase notes, not this week’s work.
