# Competitive positioning

## What people use today

- WhatsApp groups
- Paper logs and whiteboards
- Excel / Google Sheets
- Radio without a written record
- Generic tools: Slack, Teams, Notion, Monday, Trello
- Incident tools built for IT (Pagers, Jira)
- Festival-specific tools that are heavy, expensive, or dated
- Medical or security systems that do not talk to the rest of control

## The gap

None of those give a **single operational picture** with:

- fast logging
- incidents with a real timeline
- live map
- programme now/next
- RBAC
- audit
- event-type modules
- clone-and-review

## How to talk about it

> Livehelm is the control room for the event. It does not sell tickets. It does not replace radio. It replaces the pile of chats and sheets that event control cannot search, assign, or defend afterwards.

## Do not

- Name competitors in-product.
- Claim certification you do not have.
- Claim you are approved by emergency services.
- Claim AI “runs safety”.
