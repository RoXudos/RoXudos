# UK company setup

## Recommendation

Incorporate a **UK private limited company** first.

Reasons:

- Founder context and first operational market are UK event practice
- UK GDPR and ICO are the home privacy regime
- Banking, VAT, and contractor payments are simpler from a UK Ltd
- EU and US customers can still be served from a UK company with the right contracts and transfers

A US C-Corp or EU entity is a later financing or sales decision, not a day-one requirement.

## Steps

1. Choose a company name that is available at Companies House.
2. Choose a SIC code that is honest (software / information technology).
3. Incorporate online via Companies House.
4. Record people with significant control.
5. Register for Corporation Tax when HMRC writes.
6. Open a business bank account. Do not run the SaaS through a personal account.
7. Set accounting software in the company name.
8. PAYE if you pay yourself or anyone else as staff.
9. VAT: register when you approach the threshold or earlier if you want to charge VAT on B2B invoices.

## Documents to store

- Certificate of incorporation
- Memorandum and articles
- Share register
- Director ID / verification records
- Bank letters
- Insurance schedules

Store them in the company password manager / a legal folder. Not in the public git repo.

## Founder note

This file is a checklist, not legal advice. Use a UK accountant for tax and a solicitor for shareholder agreements if more than one founder or an investor appears.
