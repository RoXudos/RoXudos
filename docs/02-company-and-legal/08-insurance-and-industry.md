# Insurance and event-industry context

## Insurance for Livehelm the software company

Speak to a broker who understands SaaS + cyber.

Typical covers to ask about:

- Professional indemnity (the product is used in safety-adjacent operations)
- Cyber (including breach costs)
- Public / product liability as advised

Tell the broker, honestly:

- customers will log medical and safeguarding information
- you are not dispatching emergency services
- humans remain decision-makers
- you have RBAC and audit

## Insurance is not a product feature

Do not build an “upload your PLI certificate” module and call the event safe. Vendor document expiry is a register, not a guarantee.

## Event-industry practice

Useful reading for UK-shaped events (practice, not law by default):

- The Purple Guide (outdoor events)
- The Green Guide (crowds / sports grounds) where relevant
- Local authority / Safety Advisory Group expectations
- Charity Commission safeguarding guidance when charity mode is on

The product should help a team follow their plan. It should not claim to certify an event.

## Product copy rule

Never say “Purple Guide compliant” as a badge. Say “designed for event-control practice”.
