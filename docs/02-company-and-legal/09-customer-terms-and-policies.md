# Customer terms and policies

Have counsel write the final versions. These are the sections that must exist.

## Terms of service

- What the product is and is not (not emergency-service CAD, not clinical software, not a ticket shop)
- Customer is responsible for operational decisions on site
- Acceptable use
- Uptime target (be conservative)
- Data processing pointer to the DPA
- Limitation of liability appropriate to safety-adjacent software
- Suspension / termination
- Governing law: England and Wales unless counsel says otherwise

## Privacy policy

- Controllers vs processors
- Categories of data
- Regions
- Retention
- Rights and `privacy@`
- Subprocessors
- US / EU addenda

## Data processing agreement

- Subject matter: event operations data entered by the customer
- Duration
- Nature: hosting, backup, support
- Types of personal data
- Special category data if medical / safeguarding modules are enabled
- Customer instructions
- Subprocessor authorisation (list or mechanism)
- Deletion / return at the end
- Audit / information rights at a realistic SaaS level

## Security exhibit

Short and true:

- MFA
- encryption in transit and at rest
- RLS / tenant isolation
- logging
- backups
- access by Livehelm staff only for support, audited

## Status page

A public status URL, even if it is a simple Better Stack page.
