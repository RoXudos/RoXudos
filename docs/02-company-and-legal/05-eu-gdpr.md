# EU GDPR

Serving EU organisations from a UK company is possible. It is not automatic.

## Extra work versus UK-only

- EU GDPR is similar but not identical to UK GDPR after Brexit.
- You may need an **EU representative** if you offer the service to EU organisations and have no EU establishment.
- International transfers from EU customers to a UK processor currently benefit from UK adequacy, but you must still document processors and locations.
- If you later move data to the US by default, this becomes much harder. That is why the primary database is London.

## Practical FPR approach

1. Keep the primary database and file storage in the UK/EU.
2. Publish subprocessors with regions.
3. Sign a DPA that names Livehelm as processor.
4. Budget for an EU representative once you have EU customers, or earlier if counsel says the offering is clearly aimed at the EU.
5. Do not geo-block the EU. Do not pretend you have an EU entity you do not have.

## Cookies and marketing

If you add a public marketing site:

- avoid non-essential US ad cookies
- if you use any non-essential tracker, use a real consent banner
- product app analytics can stay off for FPR
