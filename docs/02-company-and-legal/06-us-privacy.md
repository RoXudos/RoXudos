# US privacy

The founder asked for UK + EU + US from the start. That means **US customers are allowed**, not that you become a US healthcare vendor.

## What we will not claim

- HIPAA covered entity or business associate **unless** you later sign BAAs and design for PHI. FPR medical notes are event-operations records with strict minimisation, not a hospital chart.
- FedRAMP, StateRAMP, or SOC 2 on day one.

If a US client’s medical workflow would be PHI, refuse that workflow or keep medical free-text out of the product until counsel and a BAA exist.

## State privacy laws

California CPRA and other state laws can apply once you have US personal information at scale. For FPR:

- Put a US-aware privacy notice section online.
- Honour deletion / access requests at `privacy@`.
- Do not sell personal information.
- Do not use attendee or staff data for advertising.

## Transfers into the US

Several processors may still touch the US (support tools, some email routes, a founder laptop while travelling).

- Prefer EU/UK regions on every vendor.
- Document residual US access in the subprocessor list.
- Use SCCs / UK IDTA where counsel requires them.

## US entity

Not required to take a US festival as a customer. Required later if you hire US staff, raise on a US term sheet, or want local procurement comfort.

## Sales language

Say:

> Primary data region is London. We can contract with US organisations. We are not a HIPAA product.

Do not say:

> Fully US-hosted and SOC 2 certified

until that is true.
