# People and shifts

## Steps

1. People CRUD with kind, department, contacts, emergency contact, assigned location, supervisor.
2. Permission `people.manage` vs `people.read`.
3. Shifts with start/end.
4. Volunteer is a `kind`, not a separate product. No public application form.
5. Do not show emergency contacts on Live.

## Done when

You can roster a security team and a volunteer team on the same event and filter the directory.
